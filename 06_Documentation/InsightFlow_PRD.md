\# InsightFlow

\## Enterprise Business Intelligence Platform



\### Project Vision

InsightFlow is an enterprise business intelligence web application that enables organizations to upload business datasets, automate ETL workflows, perform SQL analytics, visualize business KPIs, and generate executive reports through a modern analytics platform.
